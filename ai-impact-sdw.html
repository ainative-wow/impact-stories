The Challenge

A new team, thrown at a familiar set of problems

Team from scratch

Most members new to Allianz, the SDW domain, and the codebase.

Scattered requirements

Spread across multiple tools, with no single source of truth.

Limited transparency

Into decisions and progress across the team.

Open questions on AI

Could quality hold up? How would roles change under AI-assisted delivery?

The goal

Accelerate onboarding, improve visibility and collaboration, and establish a more efficient delivery approach — without compromising quality or accountability.
