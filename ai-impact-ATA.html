01The Situation▲
15,329 NA devices across 9 operating entities, no single fleet view. Compliance, policy, and inventory reports were pieced together by hand from Intune, SCCM, and exports, with no trend history.
02The Solution▲
Built end-to-end with Claude Code as the engineering platform, reading the Intune Graph data model and building a FastAPI and React app, shipping 90+ versions in about 3 months. Delivers live fleet analytics, an AI-driven Policy Intelligence suite (CIS audits, gap journeys, plain-English summaries), and an AI Assistant for plain-English fleet questions.
03The Impact▲
A live Dashboard, Trends, and OE Mission Control now cover all 15,329 NA devices with a full Policy Intelligence suite. The AI Assistant answers fleet and policy questions in plain English, instantly, against live data: "We went from piecing together fleet and policy reports by hand to just asking the Hub, and getting a straight answer in seconds."
